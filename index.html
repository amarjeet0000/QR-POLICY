1. Collection of your personal information and how we use it

1.1 What personal information we collect about you

(a) Our app collects the following information about you:

  • (iii) Technical logs, including device model, OS version, and crash reports via Firebase.

(b) If you contact support, we will collect your e-mail address and any information included in your correspondence.

2.1 We consider that the legal bases for using your personal information are: (a) Performance of a contract (Terms of Service); (b) Your explicit consent (for ads and camera access); (c) Necessary for our legitimate interests (growing our business via advertising revenue).
